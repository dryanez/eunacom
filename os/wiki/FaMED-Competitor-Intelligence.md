## 🏢 2. The 4 Competitor Archetypes in Germany

### 1. The High-Ticket Institute — **Freiburg International Academy (FIA)**
* **Core Offer**: Intensive 4–12 week full-time / part-time FSP and KP courses (€1,500 – €3,200+).
* **Their Funnel**:
  ```
  Meta Video Ad (Free FSP Simulation) ➔ Lead Form ➔ WhatsApp / Info Session ➔ High-Ticket Registration
  ```
* **Winning Hook Formula**:
  - *Hook (0–3s)*: „Kostenlose FSP-Prüfungssimulation: Teste deinen Wissensstand vor der echten Prüfung.“
  - *Agitation (3–15s)*: Highlight the strict evaluation criteria of the German Landesärztekammer.
  - *Pitch*: Certified courses eligible for 100% funding via *Bildungsgutschein / Jobcenter*.
* **Their Weakness**: Rigid classroom hours (clashes with hospital shifts) and extremely expensive if doctors pay out of pocket.
* **FaMED Counter-Angle**: **100% on-demand mobile practice anytime during shifts starting at only €9.99 for 7 days or €25.99 for 1 month.**

---

### 2. The 1-on-1 Medical Coaching — **Approbatio & Deutsch4Docs**
* **Core Offer**: Private tutoring packages and doctor recruitment (€60 – €90/hour or €800 – €2,000 package).
* **Their Funnel**:
  ```
  Doctor UGC Video (Exam Failure Anxiety) ➔ Free 15-Min Strategy Call ➔ Private Tutoring Package
  ```
* **Winning Hook Formula**:
  - *Hook (0–3s)*: „Warum über 60% der ausländischen Ärzte beim ersten Versuch der Fachsprachprüfung durchfallen.“
  - *Agitation (3–15s)*: Doctors know medical science, but freeze when talking to difficult German patients under time pressure.
  - *Pitch*: 1-on-1 mock exam simulations with medical coaches.
* **Their Weakness**: Prohibitively expensive per hour; stressful to schedule lessons around clinic shift work.
* **FaMED Counter-Angle**: **Unlimited AI patient simulations 24/7 with zero embarrassment for just €0.86 / day.**

---

### 3. Traditional Language Schools — **Alpha Aktiv / GoAcademy / ICF**
* **Core Offer**: B2–C1 Medical German group classes (€400 – €800/month).
* **Their Funnel**:
  ```
  Static / Carousel Ad (Syllabus PDF) ➔ Email Lead Capture ➔ Monthly Class Enrollment
  ```
* **Their Weakness**: Focuses heavily on textbook grammar and passive worksheets rather than rapid spoken conversational reflexes and real-time Arztbrief drafting.
* **FaMED Counter-Angle**: **Active voice dialogue with AI patients + instant speech scoring and automatic Arztbrief grading.**

---

### 4. General Online Platforms — **Lingoda Medical & Preply**
* **Core Offer**: Online general/medical German lessons with freelance teachers (€200 – €500/month).
* **Their Funnel**:
  ```
  Free 7-Day Trial ➔ Recurring Monthly Subscription
  ```
* **Their Weakness**: Freelance teachers rarely know official Landesärztekammer FaMED protocol cases or clinical terminology standards.
* **FaMED Counter-Angle**: **Dedicated FaMED simulator trained on 50+ official examination cases and protocols.**
